## Overview
– Implemented beam-forming and beam-steering algorithms for a Focal Plane Array (FPA) to optimize spatial resolution at the radio telescope.  
– Achieved 0.01 MHz spectral resolution using the MUSIC algorithm (MUltiple SIgnal Classification), improving frequency discrimination.  
– Developed an image-processing workflow to convert raw voltage signals into high-SNR images, improving faint source detection.
