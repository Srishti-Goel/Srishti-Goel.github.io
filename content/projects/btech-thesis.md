## Overview
– Developed a compressed sensing pipeline for accurate channel estimation and tracking in fast-fading uplink wireless channels.  
– Applied Sparse Bayesian Learning (MSBL, Temporally-Correlated SBL) for initial channel estimation, followed by Stochastic Gradient Descent for real-time channel tracking.  
– Achieved 0.1% Mean-Squared Error in signal reconstruction for SNR values from 10 to 30 dB, demonstrating robustness to noise and fading.
