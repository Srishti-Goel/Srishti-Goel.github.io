## Overview
– Investigated generative ML methods for producing synthetic Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB) maps to augment datasets for statistical studies of CMB non-Gaussianities.  
– Implemented and compared multiple architectures — GANs, VAEs, and diffusion models — on progressively complex Gaussian-based simulated datasets, from single-source patterns to multi-source distributions approximating real CMB patches.  
– Performed statistical analysis of generated outputs, identifying systematic artifacts introduced by generative models that could mimic non-Gaussian signatures absent in the training data.
