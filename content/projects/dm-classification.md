## Overview
– Simulated a dataset of distant galaxies gravitationally lensed by a foreground galaxy with various DM distributions - NFW, Isothermal, Sersic and Gaussian distributions.  
– Implemented a Logistic Regression model to classify the lensing images into the various distributions.  
– Developed a CNN model to determine the parameters of the NFW distribution.  
– Simulated the effect of the movement of the foreground galaxy with respect to the distant galaxy.
