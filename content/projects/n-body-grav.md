## Overview
– Studied the dynamic evolution of a Globular cluster of stars using MCMC simulations to understand the rate of formation or multiple-star systems in the same, and the gravitational stability.  
– Simulated the evolution of the universe in a set of co-moving coordinates starting with a random distribution of matter and dark matter along with basic Baryonic matter physics such as gas cooling and star formation  
– Simulated the effect of initial perturbations in the distributions on the formation of structure in the universe
