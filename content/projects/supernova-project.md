## Overview
– Extended an existing 1-flavor neutrino fast-flavor oscillation for supernova for heavy-flavor asymmetry, enabling more realistic modeling.  
– Performed a statistical analysis on a synthetic dataset for heavy-flavor oscillations, quantifying the effects of adding muon and tau neutrinos (effective 2-flavor and 3-flavor datasets respectively).  
– Differentiated effects of known physical phenomena like moment-hierarchy between the flavors on the distributions of the kinds of oscillations.  
– Developed a 2-level machine-learning classification pipeline to detect the signature of the fast-flavor oscillations from supernova hydrodynamic simulation data, achieving 95% accuracy
